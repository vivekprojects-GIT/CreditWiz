## Overview

Handles the FinCEN beneficial ownership certification. It works through the four ownership prongs and the single control prong, and produces the completed form for review.

> [!INFO] One of the review specialists
> The KYC Supervisor Agent delegates the FinCEN beneficial ownership stage to this agent and brings its result into the case view.

## Getting started

1. Open to all employees. Sign in with your MUFG account.
2. Find the FinCEN BO Form Agent in the AI Marketplace under **KYC Workflow**.
3. Start with one of the requests below, then adapt it to your case.

## What to give it and what you get back

**Input.** The ownership and control information on the case for the client entity.

**Output.** A completed FinCEN beneficial ownership form, covering the four ownership prongs and the control prong, ready for review.

## What you can ask

- Complete a FinCEN beneficial ownership form
- Work through the four ownership prongs for an entity
- Establish the control prong for a client

## Capabilities

- **Beneficial ownership analysis**
- **Completeness checking**
- **Audit trail**
- **Cited source annotation**

## Where it fits in the workflow

The KYC Supervisor Agent delegates the FinCEN stage to it and aggregates the result into the case view. The KYC Completeness Agent checks that this stage has reported OK before the case is closed.

## Frequently asked questions

**What are the prongs?**

Four ownership prongs and one control prong. The form covers each of them.

**Does it file the form?**

No. It produces the completed form for review.
