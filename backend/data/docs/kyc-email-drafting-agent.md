## Overview

Drafts the outbound correspondence a KYC case needs: initial outreach, requests for information, follow-up notifications and chasers. It produces a draft for a reviewer to check and send, and never sends on its own.

> [!INFO] Drafts, never sends
> Every message this agent writes goes to a KYC analyst first. The analyst checks it and sends it.

## Getting started

1. Open to all employees. Sign in with your MUFG account.
2. Find the Email Drafting Agent in the AI Marketplace under **KYC Workflow**.
3. Start with one of the requests below, then adapt it to your case.

## What to give it and what you get back

**Input.** The case and what is missing or needed from the client.

**Output.** A ready-to-review draft: an outreach email, a request for information, a follow-up or a chaser.

## What you can ask

- Draft a request-for-information letter to a client
- Draft a follow-up chase for outstanding KYC documents
- Draft an outreach email to open a KYC review

## Capabilities

- **Customer communication**
- **Summarisation**
- **Cited source annotation**

## Where it fits in the workflow

The KYC Supervisor Agent calls it when a review stage finds a gap. The draft goes to a KYC analyst, who reviews and sends it. The client's reply comes back through the AMT mailbox, where the Client Response Analyzer Agent picks it up.

## Limitations and guardrails

- It drafts; it never sends. A person checks and sends every message.
- Check each draft against the case before sending.
- This agent is in development. Check its output before relying on it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can it send the email for me?**

No. Sending is always a person's decision.

**Can I change the draft?**

Yes. Edit it as you would any draft before you send it.
