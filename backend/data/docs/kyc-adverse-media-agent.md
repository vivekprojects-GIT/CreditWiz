## Overview

Covers the adverse media and heightened risk review. It searches for financial crime coverage and regulatory actions associated with a client, and summarises what it found for the reviewer.

> [!INFO] One of the review specialists
> The KYC Supervisor Agent delegates the adverse media stage to this agent and brings its result into the case view.

## Getting started

1. Open to all employees. Sign in with your MUFG account.
2. Find the AHR / Adverse Media Agent in the AI Marketplace under **KYC Workflow**.
3. Start with one of the requests below, then adapt it to your case.

## What to give it and what you get back

**Input.** The client and the related parties on the case.

**Output.** A summary of financial crime coverage and regulatory actions found for the client, for the reviewer to assess.

## What you can ask

- Search adverse media for a client
- Find regulatory actions against an entity
- Summarise financial crime coverage for a KYC review

## Capabilities

- **Adverse media screening**
- **Investigation support**
- **Summarisation**
- **Cited source annotation**

## Where it fits in the workflow

The KYC Supervisor Agent delegates the adverse media stage to it and aggregates the result into the case view. The KYC Completeness Agent checks that this stage has reported OK before the case is closed.

## Limitations and guardrails

- It summarises what it finds. A reviewer assesses its relevance to the client.
- This agent is in development. Check its output before relying on it.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does it look for?**

Coverage of financial crime and regulatory actions associated with the client.

**Does it rate the client's risk?**

No. It summarises what it found for the reviewer, who assesses the risk.
