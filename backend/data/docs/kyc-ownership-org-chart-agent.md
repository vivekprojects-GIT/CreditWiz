## Overview

Analyses corporate structure to establish the ultimate beneficial ownership chain and the entity hierarchy behind a client, so a reviewer can see who ultimately owns and controls the entity.

> [!INFO] One of the review specialists
> The KYC Supervisor Agent delegates the ownership stage to this agent and brings its result into the case view.

## Getting started

1. Open to all employees. Sign in with your MUFG account.
2. Find the Ownership / Org Chart Agent in the AI Marketplace under **KYC Workflow**.
3. Start with one of the requests below, then adapt it to your case.

## What to give it and what you get back

**Input.** The client entity and the ownership and formation documents on the case.

**Output.** The ultimate beneficial ownership chain and the entity hierarchy, showing who owns and who controls the entity.

## What you can ask

- Establish the ultimate beneficial ownership chain for an entity
- Map the entity hierarchy behind a corporate client
- Identify who ultimately controls a client entity

## Capabilities

- **Beneficial ownership analysis**
- **Entity resolution**
- **Audit trail**
- **Cited source annotation**

## Where it fits in the workflow

The KYC Supervisor Agent delegates the ownership stage to it and aggregates the result into the case view. The KYC Completeness Agent checks that this stage has reported OK before the case is closed.

## Limitations and guardrails

- It maps ownership and control from the documents on the case. A reviewer confirms the result.
- This agent is in development. Check its output before relying on it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does it decide who the beneficial owners are?**

It proposes the chain from the documents. A reviewer confirms it.

**What if the structure has many layers?**

It maps each layer of the hierarchy so the reviewer can follow control from the client to the ultimate owners.
